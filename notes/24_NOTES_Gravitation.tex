PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Gravitation

Outside a planet, \(g(r) = g\,R^2/r^2\).

Inside a uniform sphere only the mass within radius \(r\) pulls, so the force grows linearly with \(r\).

Circular orbit: gravity supplies the centripetal force.

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

Kepler third-law form assumes a small orbiting mass around a much larger central mass.
