Work and mechanical energy

Work-energy theorem.

Kinetic friction over a distance \(d\) turns \(\mu_k N d\) of mechanical energy into heat.

Extra rise after leaving a ramp at speed \(v\) and angle \(\theta\): only the vertical part of the velocity gets used up.
