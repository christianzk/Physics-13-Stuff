PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Simple harmonic motion

SHM happens whenever the restoring force is proportional to displacement, \(F = -(\text{constant})\,x\).

Springs side by side (in parallel) add.

Pendulum in an elevator: plus when it accelerates up, minus when it accelerates down, zero in free fall.

A rolling solid sphere has kinetic energy \(\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac75 Mv^2\), so it oscillates like a mass of \(\tfrac75 M\).

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

Spring SHM is sinusoidal. Linear damping adds velocity-opposing force. Small-angle pendulum period does not depend on mass.
