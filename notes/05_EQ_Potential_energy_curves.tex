PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Potential-energy curves

$$F = -\frac{dU}{dx}$$

$$\frac{dU}{dx} = 0$$

$$U(x) = E$$

$$K_{\max} = E - U_{\min}$$

ADDITIONAL LECTURE EQUATIONS

$$\vec F=-\nabla U$$

$$F_x=-\frac{\partial U}{\partial x}$$

$$F_y=-\frac{\partial U}{\partial y}$$

$$F_z=-\frac{\partial U}{\partial z}$$
