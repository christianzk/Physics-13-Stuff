PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Thermal expansion and the first law

A heated bimetal strip curls toward the layer with the smaller \(\alpha\).

For an ideal gas, \(U\) depends only on \(T\) (the \(\tfrac32\) is for a monatomic gas), so \(\Delta U\) has the sign of \(\Delta T\), which follows \(pV\).

\(W_{\text{by}}\) is the area under the path: positive when the gas expands, negative when it's compressed.

Isothermal process.

Adiabatic process: an expanding gas cools. Adiabats are steeper than isotherms on a \(pV\) diagram.

Around a full cycle, the net work done by the gas equals the enclosed area, positive for a clockwise cycle.

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

Use absolute Kelvin temperature in gas and Carnot expressions. Sensible heat changes temperature; latent heat changes phase. Entropy in an isolated system never decreases.

HEAT ENGINES
Work output is heat absorbed minus heat expelled. Carnot is the maximum reversible efficiency between two reservoirs.

ENTROPY
For any change in state, calculate entropy along a reversible reference path.
