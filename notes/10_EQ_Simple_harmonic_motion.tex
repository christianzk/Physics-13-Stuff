PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Simple harmonic motion

$$\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = -\omega^2 x$$

$$T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$$

$$f = \frac1T$$

$$v_{\max} = \omega A$$

$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{eff}}}{m}}$$

$$k_{\text{eff}} = k_1 + k_2$$

$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g_{\text{eff}}}}$$

$$g_{\text{eff}} = g \pm a$$

$$\frac12 k_{\text{eff}}A^2 = \frac12 Mv_{\max}^2 + \frac12 I\omega_{\text{spin}}^2$$

ADDITIONAL LECTURE EQUATIONS

$$x=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)$$

$$\omega^2=\frac{k}{m}$$

$$f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$

$$v=-A\omega\sin(\omega t+\phi)$$

$$E=\frac12mv^2+\frac12kx^2$$

$$E=\frac12kA^2$$

$$f_{\rm pend}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac gL}$$

$$F_{\rm damp}=-bv$$

$$m\ddot x=-kx-b\dot x$$
