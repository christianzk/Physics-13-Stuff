PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Torque and rotational dynamics

$$v = r\omega$$

$$a_t = r\alpha$$

$$\tau = rF\sin\phi$$

$$\vec\tau = \vec r\times\vec F$$

$$\sum\tau = I\alpha$$

$$a = \frac{g}{1 + I/(mR^2)}$$

$$K_{\text{rot}} = \frac12 I\omega^2$$


$$s=r\theta$$

$$\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t$$

$$\theta=\theta_0+\omega_0t+\frac12\alpha t^2$$

$$\omega^2=\omega_0^2+2\alpha\Delta\theta$$

$$\bar\omega=\frac{\omega_0+\omega}{2}$$

$$K=\frac12 Mv_{\rm cm}^2+\frac12I\omega^2$$
