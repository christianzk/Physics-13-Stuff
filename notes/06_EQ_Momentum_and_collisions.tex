PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Momentum and collisions

$$\vec p = m\vec v$$

$$\sum\vec p_{\text{before}} = \sum\vec p_{\text{after}}$$

$$m_1\vec v_1 + m_2\vec v_2 = (m_1 + m_2)\vec v_f$$

$$\frac{K_{\text{after}}}{K_{\text{before}}} = \frac{m_1}{m_1 + m_2}$$

$$m_1v_1 = m_2v_2$$

$$\frac12 kx^2 = \frac12 m_1v_1^2 + \frac12 m_2v_2^2$$

ADDITIONAL LECTURE EQUATIONS

$$\vec J=\int\vec F\,dt=\Delta\vec p$$

$$\vec J=\vec F\Delta t$$

$$\frac{d\vec P}{dt}=\sum\vec F_{\rm ext}$$

$$x_{\rm cm}=\frac{m_1x_1+m_2x_2}{m_1+m_2}$$

$$\vec r_{\rm cm}=\frac1M\int\vec r\,dm$$

$$\vec P=M\vec v_{\rm cm}$$

$$\sum\vec F_{\rm ext}=M\vec a_{\rm cm}$$
