Momentum and collisions

Holds during a brief collision even if friction acts afterward.

Objects that stick together. In 2D, conserve \(x\) and \(y\) separately; the final direction has \(\tan\theta = p_y/p_x\).

Sticky collision with \(m_2\) at rest. Kinetic energy is lost, so never use energy conservation across this kind of collision.

Two objects pushed apart from rest by a spring.
