PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Motion with constant acceleration

$$v = v_0 + at$$

$$x = x_0 + v_0t + \frac12 at^2$$

$$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a \Delta x$$

$$x = v_{0x}t$$

$$y = y_0 + v_{0y}t - \frac12 gt^2$$

$$t_{\text{top}} = \frac{v_{0y}}{g}$$

$$h_{\max} = \frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g}$$

$$\Delta y_1(t) = \Delta y_2(t - \Delta t)$$

ADDITIONAL LECTURE EQUATIONS

$$v=\frac{dx}{dt}$$

$$a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}$$

$$\bar v=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$$

$$\bar a=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$$

$$\bar v=\frac{v_0+v}{2}$$

$$\Delta x=\bar v t$$
