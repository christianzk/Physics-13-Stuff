PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Potential-energy curves

The force points downhill on the \(U\) curve.

Equilibrium. Stable if \(d^2U/dx^2 \gt 0\) (a minimum), unstable if \(d^2U/dx^2 \lt 0\) (a maximum).

Turning points, where \(K = 0\). Motion is allowed only where \(U \le E\).

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

A conservative force is the negative spatial gradient of potential energy.
