PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Torque and rotational dynamics

A rope unwinding from radius \(r\) without slipping has \(a = r\alpha\).

Pick a positive rotation direction and use it for every torque and every acceleration.

Mass \(m\) hanging from a rope wrapped on a wheel of radius \(R\); comes from \(mg - T = ma\) and \(TR = Ia/R\).

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

Angular kinematics with constant angular acceleration parallels one-dimensional constant-acceleration motion.
