PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Motion with constant acceleration

Use it when time isn't given or asked for.

Projectiles: the horizontal speed stays constant; only the vertical motion feels gravity.

At the top of the arc, \(v_y = 0\).

Objects launched at different times: write each displacement with its own clock and set them equal.

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

Rates describe slopes of position-time and velocity-time graphs. Constant acceleration permits mean velocity from initial and final velocity.
