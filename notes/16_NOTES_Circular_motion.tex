PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Circular motion

Points toward the center: \(\sum F_{\text{toward center}} = m a_c\).

The moment of losing contact. Top of a loop: \(mg = mv^2/R\). On a dome: \(mg\cos\theta = mv^2/R\).

Conical pendulum. Together these give \(\cos\theta = g/(l\omega^2)\).

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

The radial net force points toward the center, even though velocity is tangent.
