PHYSICS 13 - EQUATIONS
Moments of inertia

$$I = \sum m_i r_i^2$$

$$I_{\text{rod, center}} = \frac{1}{12}ML^2$$

$$I_{\text{rod, end}} = \frac13 ML^2$$

$$I_{\text{hoop}} = MR^2$$

$$I_{\text{disk}} = \frac12 MR^2$$

$$I_{\text{sphere}} = \frac25 MR^2$$

$$I = I_{\text{cm}} + Md^2$$
