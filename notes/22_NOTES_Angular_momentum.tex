PHYSICS 13 - NOTES
Angular momentum

Point mass: \(L = mvr_\perp = mr^2\omega\).

Add every piece, for example a hanging mass plus the wheel it unwinds from.

Angular momentum is conserved when there's no net external torque.

Gyroscopes: the spin axis tips toward \(\vec\tau\), not toward the push.

LECTURE SUMMARY ADDITIONS

External torque produces the time rate of change of angular momentum. Zero net external torque conserves angular momentum.
